Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough. There is no mission replay and no difficulty setting, but free-roam carries on after the story, so almost everything can be finished after the credits. PowerPyx puts the whole run at about 200 hours.

Watch out: one trophy can be missed

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot across Leonida) is the only trophy the guides flag as potentially missable. The first meditation is offered at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, and that spot becomes permanently unavailable after the chapter ends. Meditate there the first time the game offers it, and keep a save from early Chapter 1. Source: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap.

Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off until the platinum pops.
